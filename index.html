The problem

Public cloud wasn't built for proprietary science

AI now touches every stage of drug development, from target identification and molecule design to trial optimization. Running these workloads on shared public cloud creates two problems.

Your IP leaves your control

Proprietary compound libraries, research data, and trained models end up on infrastructure you don't own and can't fully inspect.

GPU costs become unpredictable

Usage-based billing grows with every model you scale, which makes research budgets hard to plan and harder to defend.

What is Private AI Infrastructure?

Private AI Infrastructure is dedicated GPU compute, networking, and storage that runs only your workloads, in an isolated environment you control. Unlike shared public cloud, no other tenant runs on the hardware, your data stays in your environment, and pricing is fixed rather than metered. OneSource Cloud designs, deploys, and operates it for you from U.S. data centers.
